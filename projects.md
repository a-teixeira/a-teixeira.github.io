### CoronaNet — PHSM data harmonization
**TUM / CoronaNet** · 2021–2023  
Stack: research data workflows · quality control · training · R / structured coding

Global effort to **harmonize** public health and social measure (PHSM) / government COVID-19 response data so it is comparable for analysis.

**My role (acknowledged in the project’s scholarly output)**  
- Onboarded and trained research assistants on the **data harmonization** process.  
- Monitored harmonization quality and gave structured **feedback on errors** to RAs.  
- Took part in the **summer 2021 pilot** of the harmonization workflow.  
- Supported integration/harmonization work so heterogeneous sources could sit in one analysis-ready schema.

**Recognition**  
Named acknowledgement for onboarding/training and for monitoring harmonization errors in:  
*Harmonizing government responses to the COVID-19 pandemic* (project article; acknowledgements).  

Links: [CoronaNet](https://www.coronanet-project.org) · [Article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02881-x)
