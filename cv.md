## Experience

### CoronaNet Research Project — Technical University of Munich (HfP)
**Munich, Germany** · Jul 2021 – Oct 2023  
*Research internship (500h) → Research Assistant / Country Expert → Data Integration & Data Science Analyst · Regional management support*

CoronaNet builds a fine-grained, publicly available dataset of government COVID-19 policy responses worldwide for statistical and qualitative research ([coronanet-project.org](https://www.coronanet-project.org)).

- Completed a **500-hour** research internship at the Chair of International Relations; retained as staff after becoming a core contributor working with the principal investigators.
- **Acknowledged by name** in the project article on harmonizing government COVID-19 responses — for RA **onboarding/training** on harmonization and for **monitoring and feedback on harmonization errors**.
- Participant in the **2021 pilot** of the manual harmonization process.
- Collected and coded policy evidence on an ongoing weekly basis (country expert), contributing to a project that documented **180,000+** policy records globally; personal focus included high-volume collection and rigorous validation (incl. Luxembourg and Brazil).
- Led **data integration**: ingested and harmonized feeds from external datasets into CoronaNet’s database, working across heterogeneous structures and large, evolving tables.
- Served as **Data Science Analyst**: supported technical management of the living policy dataset and the infrastructure needed to keep collection and updates reliable for internal and external research use.
- As **regional manager / project management support**, monitored research assistants’ output and **data quality**, enforced coding standards, and supported coordination in a network of **200+** RAs (trainings, weekly regional and project-wide meetings).
- Used structured coding workflows (custom **Qualtrics** instrument), spreadsheet/database practice, and **R**-oriented analysis/reporting; emphasized problem-solving across different data architectures.
- Produced clear updates for coordination and stakeholders; helped onboard and mentor juniors on coding procedures and quality expectations.

*Reference: Luca Messerschmidt, Principal Investigator, CoronaNet / TUM (available on request).*
