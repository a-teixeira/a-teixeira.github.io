<h2 id="role">Role &amp; context</h2>
<p><strong>TUM / Hochschule für Politik</strong> · approx. Jul 2021 – Oct 2023 · internship (500h) → hired contributor. Worked on <a href="https://www.coronanet-project.org">CoronaNet</a>, a fine-grained public dataset of government COVID-19 policy responses for research.</p>

<h2 id="problem">Problem</h2>
<p>Policy information was scattered across sources, formats, and languages. For comparable research, measures had to be <strong>collected, coded, validated, and harmonized</strong> across countries and time — with many research assistants in the loop and a living dataset that kept changing.</p>

<h2 id="responsabilities">Responsabilities</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>500-hour research internship</strong>, then continued as a <strong>hired contributor</strong> with the principal investigators.</li>
<li><strong>Country expert:</strong> weekly structured coding (Qualtrics), validation, coordination meetings.</li>
<li><strong>Data integration / harmonization:</strong> brought external dataset feeds into CoronaNet; kept large, evolving tables consistent for analysis.</li>
<li><strong>Data Science Analyst support:</strong> helped with technical management of the living dataset and reliable update paths for research use.</li>
<li><strong>Regional / PM support:</strong> monitored work and <strong>data quality</strong> across a network of <strong>200+ RAs</strong> — onboarding, coding standards, feedback on errors.</li>
<li><strong>R:</strong> data manipulation, descriptive analysis, and reporting-oriented / automated steps; emphasis on process quality and repeatable workflows.</li>
<li>Contributed to the <strong>summer 2021 pilot</strong> of the harmonization workflow.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="recognition">Recognition</h2>
<p>Named in the acknowledgements of the project article on harmonizing government COVID-19 responses — for <strong>onboarding and training RAs on data harmonization</strong> and for <strong>monitoring and feedback on harmonization errors</strong>.</p>
<p><em>Cheng, C., Messerschmidt, L., et al.</em> Related output: <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02881-x" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Scientific Data / Nature portfolio article</a> — acknowledgement.</p> 
<p>Reference letter: Luca Messerschmidt, PI — available on request.</p>
