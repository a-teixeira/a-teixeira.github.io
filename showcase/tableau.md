<div class="viz-block" id="f1">
  <h2>Featured 1 — Map of Meaning (profitability)</h2>
  <p class="viz-meta">Tableau · dashboard · profitability · geography</p>
  <p><strong>Problem.</strong> Which regions/segments sell a lot but earn little (or lose money)?<br>
  <strong>Approach.</strong> Interactive Map of Meaning combining sales volume and profit so low-profit / high-sales areas stand out.<br>
  <strong>Result.</strong> Geographic profitability story — where to investigate discounts, mix, or cost.<br>
  <a href="https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/augusto.teixeira/viz/WhichofoursalesarelessprofitableMapofMeaning/Dashboard1" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open dashboard</a></p>
</div>

<div class="viz-block" id="f2">
  <h2>Featured 2 — Total Sales by Category over Time</h2>
  <p class="viz-meta">Tableau · time series · category performance</p>
  <p><strong>Problem.</strong> How do category sales evolve — growth, seasonality, relative strength?<br>
  <strong>Approach.</strong> Category × time view of total sales to compare trajectories.<br>
  <strong>Result.</strong> Clear trend comparison (“what’s growing vs flat”).<br>
  <a href="https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/augusto.teixeira/viz/TotalSalesbyCategoryoverTime/TotalSalesbyCategoryoverTime" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open viz</a></p>
</div>

<div class="viz-block" id="f3">
  <h2>Featured 3 — Superstore: losing or winning?</h2>
  <p class="viz-meta">Tableau · loss patterns · Superstore</p>
  <p><strong>Problem.</strong> Is the business winning on profit — and where do losses cluster?<br>
  <strong>Approach.</strong> Pattern-of-losses view separating winning vs losing pockets.<br>
  <strong>Result.</strong> Where profitability breaks even when sales look fine.<br>
  <a href="https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/augusto.teixeira/viz/SuperstoreDatasetareweloosingorwinnning/PatternofLosses" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open viz</a></p>
</div>

<div class="viz-block" id="f4">
  <h2>Featured 4 — Customer Scatterplot</h2>
  <p class="viz-meta">Tableau · customer analytics · scatter</p>
  <p><strong>Problem.</strong> How do customers distribute on key metrics (e.g. sales vs profit)?<br>
  <strong>Approach.</strong> Customer scatterplot for outliers and segments.<br>
  <strong>Result.</strong> Who to protect, who to fix, who is noise.<br>
